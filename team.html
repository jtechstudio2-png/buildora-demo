Governance

How decisions are made and checked.

Our governance framework separates tender approval, project delivery, and independent assurance.

Board of directors

Five members including two independent non-executives. Meets quarterly; audit and HSE committees report directly to it.

Tender committee

Reviews risk, contract terms, and margin on every bid above USD 10m before submission.

Project reviews

Monthly executive reviews of programme, cost-to-complete, quality, and HSE on every live project.

Independent assurance

Annual third-party audits of the ISO management system, financial statements, and worker welfare.

Services

Main contracting, design & build, and civil works.

We work under the procurement route that suits your project — lump-sum, re-measured, cost-plus, design & build, or EPC — on FIDIC, NEC, or bespoke conditions of contract.

Capabilities

Six service lines. One integrated management system.

All services are delivered under our ISO 9001, 14001, and 45001 certified management system and reported through the same project controls platform.

01 / PRE-CONSTRUCTION

Pre-construction & estimating

Early contractor involvement, buildability and value-engineering reviews, BOQ pricing, logistics plans, and Primavera P6 baseline programmes.

02 / MAIN CONTRACTING

General contracting

Single-point responsibility for substructure, superstructure, envelope, MEP coordination, and finishes, typically under FIDIC Red Book.

03 / ADVISORY

Construction management

Client-side package procurement, cost and programme control, and contractor coordination for developers and fund managers.

04 / DESIGN & BUILD

Design & build / EPC

Design development from concept or Stage 3 through IFC drawings and construction under FIDIC Yellow or Silver Book.

05 / INTERIORS

Fit-out & refurbishment

Cat A and Cat B fit-out, hotel and hospital refurbishment, and phased works in occupied, operational buildings.

06 / CIVIL

Infrastructure & civil works

Highways and interchanges, stormwater and sewerage networks, utility diversions, and enabling works for master plans.

Service · Main contracting

General contracting.

Single-point responsibility for building your project to the employer's design — one contract, one baseline programme, and one team accountable for safety, quality, cost, and time.

Overview

As main contractor, Buildora takes responsibility for constructing the works to the employer's drawings and specification, typically under FIDIC Red Book (Conditions of Contract for Construction) on a lump-sum or re-measured basis. We self-perform formwork, rebar, concrete, blockwork, and structural steel, and procure MEP, façade, lifts, and specialist packages from a pre-qualified supply chain of more than 900 subcontractors and suppliers.

Our general contracting teams have delivered B+G+40 towers, 140-villa communities, 220-key hotels, and 64,000 m² office campuses — on fast-track programmes, in summer conditions, and alongside occupied buildings.

Scope we deliver

  • Site establishment, hoarding, temporary works design, and logistics
  • Piling, shoring, dewatering, and substructure
  • Reinforced and post-tensioned concrete frames; structural steel
  • Envelope: curtain wall, cladding, glazing, and roofing interfaces
  • MEP coordination, combined services drawings, and builder's work
  • Architectural finishes, joinery, and external works and landscaping
  • Testing & commissioning, authority inspections, and completion certificates

How we deliver

  1. Tender. Drawing and specification review, RFIs, value-engineering alternatives, a priced BOQ, method statements, and a tender programme.
  2. Mobilisation (weeks 0–6). Project execution plan, HSE and quality plans, inspection and test plans (ITPs), long-lead procurement, and a Level 3 baseline programme in Primavera P6.
  3. Construction. Weekly progress against baseline, look-ahead programmes, RFI/NCR registers, monthly interim payment applications, and earned-value reporting.
  4. Completion & handover. Snagging and de-snagging, T&C, authority NOCs and completion certificates, O&M manuals, as-built drawings, and asset data.
  5. Defects liability period. A dedicated aftercare team with agreed response times for the full DLP, typically 12 months.

Frequently asked questions

Which forms of contract do you accept?

FIDIC 1999 and 2017 Red, Yellow, and Silver Books are our most common forms, along with the FIDIC MDB Harmonised Edition for DFI-funded projects in Africa, NEC4, and bespoke employer forms with agreed amendments.

What bonds and guarantees can you provide?

We routinely provide tender bonds, performance bonds (typically 10%), advance payment guarantees, and retention bonds through our regional banking partners.

How do you manage design changes during construction?

Every instruction is logged, assessed for cost and time impact, and submitted as a variation proposal before work proceeds, unless the employer instructs otherwise in writing.

Can you take on design responsibility mid-project?

Yes. We can novate the design team or take on contractor's design portions for specific packages such as façade, MEP, or temporary works.

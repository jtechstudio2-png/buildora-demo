Early careers

Graduate Engineer Programme.

A two-year rotational programme for civil, structural, mechanical, and electrical engineering and quantity surveying graduates. You'll complete four six-month placements across site, design management, planning, and commercial — with a mentor, a chartership plan, and a permanent role at the end.

  • Intake each September in Dubai, Doha, Riyadh, and Nairobi
  • Sponsored chartership with ICE, IStructE, RICS, or CIBSE
  • 48 graduates in the 2025 cohort, from 14 universities
  • Summer internships for penultimate-year students

How we recruit

A clear process, typically four weeks.

Every applicant receives a response. We never charge candidates fees at any stage — if anyone asks you for payment in Buildora's name, please report it to us.

Application

Send your CV quoting the job reference. Acknowledgement within five working days.

Screening call

A 30-minute call with our talent team about experience, availability, and expectations.

Technical interview

With the hiring manager and a senior engineer, including a project case discussion.

Offer & onboarding

Written offer, visa and mobilisation support, and a structured site induction.

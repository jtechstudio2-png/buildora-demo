Who we are

A main contractor built on programme certainty.

Founded in Dubai in 2001, Buildora has grown into a 2,800-strong building and civil engineering contractor with regional offices in Doha, Riyadh, Nairobi, and Lagos. We self-perform concrete, structural steel, and finishing works, and manage specialist MEP and façade packages through a pre-qualified supply chain.

Our clients include master developers, sovereign-backed entities, hospitality operators, and multinational occupiers who need a contractor that understands local authority approvals, climate, and labour conditions — and reports to international standards.

  • Grade A / first-category contractor registrations in the UAE, Qatar, and Saudi Arabia
  • Experienced on FIDIC Red, Yellow, and Silver Book contracts, and design & build
  • In-house BIM team coordinating to LOD 400 for MEP-intensive projects
